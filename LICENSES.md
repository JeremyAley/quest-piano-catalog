# Licences and credits

Each song keeps the licence of its source. The `license`, `attribution` and `source_url` fields of every song in `catalog.json` are authoritative.

All files here are adaptations: the piano part was taken from the source MIDI file, written as one track per hand, and reduced to easier difficulty levels (1 melody only; 2 melody and bass; 3 simplified; 4 full). Where a source licence requires it (share-alike), these adaptations are shared under that same licence.

The sheet music pages are engraved from each level's own notes, so they are part of the same adaptation and carry the song's licence and credit. They were rendered with [Verovio](https://www.verovio.org) (LGPL; its output is not covered by that licence) using the Leipzig music font (SIL Open Font License).

## Mutopia Project

Songs whose `attribution` ends in "Mutopia Project" come from the [Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org), typeset by the volunteer named in the attribution ("Typeset by <name>, Mutopia Project"). Each piece's licence is given in its `license` field and on its Mutopia page (`source_url`):

- **Public Domain**: free to use, adapt and share.
- **Creative Commons Attribution** ([CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)): credit the typesetter and the Mutopia Project.
- **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike** ([2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/), [3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), [4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)): credit the typesetter and the Mutopia Project; the adaptations here are shared under the same licence version.

Mutopia also supplies the carols and hymns taken from 19th-century hymnals (four voices: soprano, alto, tenor, bass). They were reduced to two hands (soprano and alto to the right hand, tenor and bass to the left) before the difficulty levels were made; the hymnal settings are public domain, and the reductions carry each piece's own licence as above.

## PDMX (scores from MuseScore)

Songs whose `attribution` mentions PDMX come from [PDMX](https://zenodo.org/records/15571083) (Long et al., "PDMX: A Large-Scale Public Domain MusicXML Dataset for Symbolic Music Processing"), a dataset of MuseScore scores that their uploaders marked public domain or CC0. The dataset is licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); this credit is given as it requires, and each song's `attribution` names the MuseScore user who engraved the score.

Uploaders' labels were not taken on trust. A score was only included when the composition is public domain (composer died before 1955 and the work was published before 1930), the score is the original piano work or a period setting rather than a modern arrangement, nothing in its metadata points to a modern arranger, film, game or recording, and the work was matched to IMSLP or Wikidata. Pieces that failed any check were left out.

## House arrangements (CC0)

Songs whose `attribution` begins "Arranged by Quest Piano Trainer (house arrangement)" are our own piano arrangements of public-domain melodies: the melody from a public-domain source, the left hand generated from chord symbols. They are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): use them for anything, no credit needed.
